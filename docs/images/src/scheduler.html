Scheduler and safety
Every job has a deadline
OBD polling and the model each get a period
Each run is timed
response time, CPU time, misses, and stalls (3 periods, or 10 s for the model)
Load is checked
utilisation against the rate-monotonic bound, once a minute
A hung step is caught
the watchdog restarts the board
Repeated bad restarts
safe mode: model off, logging stays on
Saved every 10 s
the drive closes 15 s after the engine stops answering, or when BOOT is held
OpenCarMind treats the board as a soft real-time system: every periodic job is timed against its deadline, load is checked with classic schedulability tests (Liu and Layland 1973, Joseph and Pandya 1986), and a hang can never stop logging for good.
