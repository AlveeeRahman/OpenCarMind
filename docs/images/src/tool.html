OpenCarMind, the tool
Car engine computer
answers OBD-II requests
BLE OBD-II adapter
ELM327-style, with a Bluetooth LE chip
ESP32-S3 board
reads, scores and logs every row, and shows fuel economy
AI model on the chip
manoeuvre class and 3 estimates
Session files on the SD card
saved every 10 s, closed when the engine stops
Desktop app on your computer
gets each drive over your Wi-Fi, or reads the card
No account and no cloud. Every OBD row is logged and scored on the chip. The card is saved every 10 s and the drive closes 15 s after the engine stops answering, so you can pull the plug when the screen says "Safe to turn off". At home, a paired board sends the new data to the desktop app on your own computer.
