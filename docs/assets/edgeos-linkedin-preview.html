OpenCarMind
EdgeOS 2.0

A small operating-system layer for
on-chip AI, on the ESP-Mosaico

ESP32-S31 ESP-Mosaico: 480 x 480 AMOLED, 32 MB PSRAM
2 models on the chip, both with an exact self-test
Learns on the chip: fuel use, airflow, tank and your swipes
Model admission: 4.2 ms against a 1,000 ms deadline
Boot health check, battery protection, weak adapters ignored
RAM above 60 KB in every phase, lowest 81 KB
16 touch apps, light and dark themes, 128 MB NAND logs
