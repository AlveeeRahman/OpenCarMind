# The models: CarMind R.E.V and E.V

Both boards carry two on-board models. CarMind E.V is fixed and tested. CarMind R.E.V keeps learning in your car: the board updates part of the model from what your car reports while you drive. The inputs, outputs and files of each are in [Model files](../reference/model-files.md), and the scores in [Model results](../research/model-results.md).
