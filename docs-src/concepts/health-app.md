# Health app

The Health app is new in OpenCarMind EdgeOS 2.1.0. It shows the AI services in plain words: one card per page with a colour, a word, what is wrong, what it means and what to do. Every card and button is described in the [manual's Health section](../manuals/edgeos-2.1.0/apps.md#health).
