Fuel

What the drives used, and your fill-ups. The board compares the fuel you pump with the fuel it counted and corrects its figures with one factor. Fill to the pump's automatic click-off for the best result; the second full fill gives the first correction.

Board

Connect the board to this computer with its USB cable. Everything is set up from here, so the SD card stays in the board. Do it with the car parked: the board refuses changes while it is talking to the car.

Connect

Pair this board with this app

The board shows a 6-digit code on its screen for 2 minutes. Typing it here proves you have that board in front of you. You only do this once per board.

On-board model

CarMind R.E.V is about half the size of E.V and keeps learning your car while you drive: its fuel estimate and the engine size its torque estimate uses. CarMind E.V is the fixed, tested model. New boards start on R.E.V since 2.5.1, and the switch is one click either way.

Connect to see which model runs.

Wi-Fi networks on the board

The board uses Wi-Fi to set its clock (and, once the upload is reworked, to send its drives to this app). It joins 2.4 GHz networks only and keeps passwords in its flash as plain text, so a phone hotspot or a guest network is the better choice.

    Drives on the card
