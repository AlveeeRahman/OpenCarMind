Overview

Every drive read from the SD card, on this computer only. Distances are estimated from the OBD speed signal.

Put the SD card in this computer, then choose its obd folder, its sessions folder, or any folder of sNNNNNN.csv files.

Card and Wi-Fi

The board sends its drives to this app over your Wi-Fi every 10 seconds while the car is parked. Set it up once by writing a few files to the SD card.

    Set up the card

    Take the SD card out of the board, put it in this computer and choose it here. The board reads these files when it starts, and deletes the Wi-Fi and pairing files once it has stored them.

    Wi-Fi networks

    2.4 GHz only. The board keeps passwords in its flash as plain text, so a phone hotspot or a guest network is the better choice.
