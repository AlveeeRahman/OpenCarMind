Overview

Every drive read from the SD card, on this computer only. Distances are estimated from the OBD speed signal.

Put the SD card in this computer, then choose its obd folder, its sessions folder, or any folder of sNNNNNN.csv files.

Board

This version reads the SD card only. It cannot talk to the board yet.

What is not built

Connecting to the board over Wi-Fi needs a pairing service in the firmware, and the firmware does not have one. Changing the board's settings over USB needs a settings console in the firmware, and that does not exist either. Until both exist, take the card out of the board and open it here.

Flashing the board

Flash the firmware from the release page with esptool. The steps are in the project's README.
